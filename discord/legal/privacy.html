Privacy Policy

Parcelwood (Discord Activity) · Effective November 2, 2026

Parcelwood is operated by DogcanAmee, an individual based in the United States ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the Parcelwood Discord Activity collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. Contact: 213282257+DogcanAmee@users.noreply.github.com.

Information we collect

  • Discord account information. When you start the game, Discord asks you to authorize the identify scope. We receive your Discord user ID and display name. We do not receive your email address. We store your Discord user ID; your display name is only used to show your name in the game and is not stored in our database.
  • Game account and save. We create a game account linked to your Discord user ID and store your game progress (for example coins, gems, collected items and town progress) so it can be restored on your devices. The account uses a randomly generated placeholder address that is not a real email address; no email is ever sent to it.
  • Purchase records. If paid items are offered and you buy one, Discord processes the payment. We receive and store the purchase (entitlement) ID, your Discord user ID, the item, its list price, whether it was a test purchase, and the dates it was recorded, claimed or refunded, along with your in-game gem balance history. We never receive or store your payment card details.
  • Age check. Before signing in, the game asks for your birth month and year. Only the pass/fail result is kept on your device; the birth date is not stored or sent to us. Players under 13 cannot play and nothing is stored for them.
  • On your device. The game keeps settings and a local copy of your save in your browser or Discord app storage.
  • Technical logs. Our hosting providers may record standard request information such as IP address, browser type and time of request for security and reliability.

How we use information

  • To sign you in, save and restore your progress, and deliver items you bought.
  • To handle refunds, prevent fraud or duplicate deliveries, and keep accounting records.
  • To keep the game secure and working, and to respond to your requests.

We do not show ads, we do not use third-party analytics or advertising trackers, and we do not sell or share your personal information for advertising.

Service providers

  • Discord — sign-in, the Activity platform and payments. See Discord's Privacy Policy.
  • Supabase — database and server functions (United States).
  • Vercel — hosting of the game files.

These providers process information on our behalf to run the game. We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect the rights and safety of players or the service.

Retention and deletion

We keep your game account and save until you delete them. You can delete your account from the account page in the game, or by emailing us. Deleting your account removes your game account, cloud save and the link to your Discord account. Purchase records, including the Discord user ID they were made with, are kept after deletion for accounting, refund and fraud-prevention purposes.

Your rights

You may ask us to access, correct or delete your personal information by emailing 213282257+DogcanAmee@users.noreply.github.com. Depending on where you live (for example California or the European Economic Area), you may have additional rights under local law; we will respond to verified requests as required. We will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.

Children

The game is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has provided information to us, contact us and we will delete it.

Security and international transfers

Data is stored in the United States. We use access controls and encrypted connections, but no system is perfectly secure.
